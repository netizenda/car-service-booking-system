# User Interview Questions

## Purpose

The purpose of these interviews is to understand how potential
users currently interact with car service businesses, identify
problems in the existing appointment process, and validate
requirements for the proposed Car Service Booking and Management
System.

## Questions

1. How do you currently book or manage car service appointments?

2. What problems or inconveniences do you experience during
   the appointment booking process?

3. How do you usually find out which appointment dates and
   times are available?

4. Would it be useful to see available appointment time slots
   online before making a booking? Why or why not?

5. Would you like to see service descriptions, estimated
   duration, and prices before booking?

6. Would it be useful to track the current status of the
   service, for example:
   Pending, Confirmed, In Progress, and Completed?

7. Would having a service history for each vehicle be useful
   to you? Why?

8. What other functionality would you expect from an online
   car service booking system?
