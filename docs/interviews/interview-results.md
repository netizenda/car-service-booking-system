User Interview Results
Interview 1

Date: 22 October 2026

Participant type: Car owner #1

Format: In-person interview

Question 1
How do you currently book or manage car service appointments?

Answer
Usually I just call the service center directly or send a message via WhatsApp to the administrator. If it's a small issue or regular maintenance, I sometimes drive up to the shop in person without booking and wait in line until someone is free.

Question 2
What problems or inconveniences do you experience during the appointment booking process?

Answer
The main issue is communication delays. Sometimes the manager doesn't pick up or takes hours to reply on WhatsApp because they are busy on the shop floor. Also, twice I arrived for my booked time only to find that two cars were scheduled for the same lift simultaneously, so I had to wait around for nearly an hour.

Question 3
How do you usually find out which appointment dates and times are available?

Answer
I have to ask the administrator directly during a phone call or wait for them to text back available options. It usually takes several messages back and forth to match their schedule with mine.

Question 4
Would it be useful to see available appointment time slots online before making a booking? Why or why not?

Answer
Yes, absolutely. It would save a lot of time for both parties. I could open the app or website after working hours, pick an open evening or weekend slot, and confirm it instantly without needing to talk to anyone.

Question 5
Would you like to see service descriptions, estimated duration, and prices before booking?

Answer
Yes, transparency is very important. I want to know in advance how much oil change or brake replacement will cost and roughly how many hours my car will be in the shop so I can plan my day accordingly.

Question 6
Would it be useful to track the current status of the service, for example: Pending, Confirmed, In Progress, and Completed?

Answer
Yes, definitely. Right now, I have to call them to ask if the car is ready or still being repaired. Real-time status tracking would give me peace of mind, and I’d know exactly when to leave home to pick up my vehicle.

Question 7
Would having a service history for each vehicle be useful to you? Why?

Answer
Very useful. I often forget when I last changed the cabin filter, spark plugs, or transmission fluid. Having a digital log of all previous work and mileage records would help maintain the car properly and make it much easier to prove good maintenance when I decide to sell the car.

Question 8
What other functionality would you expect from an online car service booking system?

Answer
Automated push notifications or SMS reminders a few hours before the appointment, as well as an option to easily reschedule or cancel an appointment online if my plans change.

Interview 2

Date: 22 October 2026

Participant type: Car owner #2

Format: In-person interview

Question 1
How do you currently book or manage car service appointments?

Answer
I mostly use phone calls to schedule visits with my regular mechanic. If I need specialized work like wheel alignment, I use 2GIS or Instagram to find a shop and call their hotline.

Question 2
What problems or inconveniences do you experience during the appointment booking process?

Answer
It's frustrating when you call during business hours and nobody answers because the receptionist is away or handling customers at the counter. Also, prices quoted over the phone are often vague, and the final bill ends up being higher due to added labor costs that were not clearly explained upfront.

Question 3
How do you usually find out which appointment dates and times are available?

Answer
Only by talking to the manager on the phone. They look through a paper notebook or spreadsheet and suggest a couple of times.

Question 4
Would it be useful to see available appointment time slots online before making a booking? Why or why not?

Answer
Yes, very useful. It eliminates phone tag and lets me visually evaluate all open slots for the week to choose what fits my calendar best.

Question 5
Would you like to see service descriptions, estimated duration, and prices before booking?

Answer
Yes. Knowing estimated prices and duration upfront builds trust and helps avoid unexpected charges when paying the invoice.

Question 6
Would it be useful to track the current status of the service, for example: Pending, Confirmed, In Progress, and Completed?

Answer
Yes. It prevents unnecessary phone calls to the service center to check if the work has started or if the car is ready for pickup.

Question 7
Would having a service history for each vehicle be useful to you? Why?

Answer
Yes. Keeping physical paper receipts in the glove box is inconvenient and easy to lose. A digital history attached to my car profile allows me to track maintenance intervals and expense history effortlessly.

Question 8
What other functionality would you expect from an online car service booking system?

Answer
An online payment option or advance deposit, and a feature to upload photo/video notes or audio comments describing custom issues (like a strange noise under the hood) before arriving.

Interview 3

Date: 22 October 2026

Participant type: Mechanic

Format: In-person interview

Question 1
How are customer appointments currently managed?

Answer
The front desk administrator gives us a printed daily job sheet or verbally tells us which car is coming next and what job needs to be done. We work on lifts assigned to us.

Question 2
What problems or inconveniences do you experience during the appointment booking process?

Answer
The biggest issue on our side is overbooking and incorrect job time estimation. Administrators sometimes schedule complex diagnostic tasks into a short 30-minute window, causing delays for all subsequent clients and creating unnecessary stress on the shop floor.

Question 3
How do you usually find out which appointment dates and times are available?

Answer
We check the administrator's main scheduling board or ask the shop manager. Mechanics don't manage customer bookings directly.

Question 4
Would it be useful to see available appointment time slots online before making a booking? Why or why not?

Answer
Yes, because if clients can see realistic work time frames and slot limits per lift, it will distribute the workload evenly throughout the day rather than having five cars show up at once in the morning.

Question 5
Would you like to see service descriptions, estimated duration, and prices before booking?

Answer
Yes, standardized service descriptions and accurate estimated durations are essential for us. It sets clear expectations for clients and ensures we are given enough time to perform quality repair work.

Question 6
Would it be useful to track the current status of the service, for example: Pending, Confirmed, In Progress, and Completed?

Answer
Yes, updating statuses (e.g., changing from 'In Progress' to 'Completed') directly from a tablet or terminal in the bay would immediately inform both the administrator and client without stopping work to explain things in person.

Question 7
Would having a service history for each vehicle be useful to you? Why?

Answer
Extremely useful. When a car comes in, knowing its repair history, past diagnostic codes, and previously replaced parts saves us hours of troubleshooting time

Question 8
What other functionality would you expect from an online car service booking system?

Answer
An internal mechanics' interface to attach photos of worn/damaged parts to the job order so administrators can show them to clients for approval before proceeding with extra repairs.

Interview 4

Date: 22 October 2026

Participant type: Car service administrator

Format: In-person interview

Question 1
How are customer appointments currently managed?

Answer
I manually manage all bookings using a combination of a Excel spreadsheet, paper logbooks, and direct WhatsApp messages/calls with clients.

Question 2
What problems or inconveniences do you experience during the appointment booking process?

Answer
Peak hours are chaotic. I have to answer phone calls, reply to messages, greet walk-in clients, and issue receipts all at the same time. Double bookings happen occasionally due to human error, and client no-shows without prior notice waste valuable lift time.

Question 3
How do you usually find out which appointment dates and times are available?

Answer
I constantly check our master spreadsheet and coordinate with mechanics to see which lift is free and how fast they are progressing with current tasks.

Question 4
Would it be useful to see available appointment time slots online before making a booking? Why or why not?

Answer
Yes, it would reduce my daily call volume by at least 50%. Clients could self-serve and pick available times without interrupting my customer service duties at the desk.

Question 5
Would you like to see service descriptions, estimated duration, and prices before booking?
Answer

Yes. Having pre-configured service packages with standard durations and prices helps prevent misunderstandings about costs and keeps our schedule structured.

Question 6
Would it be useful to track the current status of the service, for example: Pending, Confirmed, In Progress, and Completed?
Answer

Yes, it keeps the workflow organized. It helps us monitor throughput in real time, follow up on pending confirmations, and notify owners immediately as soon as a job is marked 'Completed'.

Question 7
Would having a service history for each vehicle be useful to you? Why?
Answer

Yes. It makes customer service much more professional. When a returning customer calls, I can instantly look up their vehicle history, see past recommendations, and build long-term trust.

Question 8
What other functionality would you expect from an online car service booking system?
Answer

An admin dashboard with daily schedule overview, automatic client reminders via WhatsApp/SMS to minimize no-shows, and basic reporting analytics (e.g., daily revenue, lift utilization rates, most requested services).
