# Literature Review Sources

## Purpose

This document contains the sources selected for the literature review
of the project "Design and Development of a Web-Based Car Service
Booking and Management System".

The selected literature covers online appointment scheduling,
service management, time-slot allocation, appointment reminders,
role-based access control, REST API architecture, and automotive
service management.

The final literature review will use at least 12 sources, including
at least 8 scientific publications.

---

## Scientific Publications

### S1. Technology and Access to Healthcare with Different Scheduling Systems

**Author:** L. Manarte  
**Year:** 2024  
**Publication:** Healthcare Informatics Research, 30(3), 194–205  
**DOI:** 10.4258/hir.2024.30.3.194  
**Type:** Scientific journal article / Scoping review  

**Topic:**  
Online appointment scheduling systems and digital access to services.

**Relevance to the project:**  
This source is useful for understanding the benefits and limitations
of online scheduling systems. It supports the idea of replacing or
supplementing manual appointment processes with a web-based booking
system.
