# Existing Systems Selected for Comparison

## Purpose

This document identifies existing automotive workshop and
car-service management systems that are relevant to the proposed
project "Design and Development of a Web-Based Car Service Booking
and Management System".

Four existing systems were selected:

1. AutoLeap
2. Tekmetric
3. Shopmonkey
4. Shop-Ware

The systems were selected because they provide functionality
related to automotive service scheduling, customer and vehicle
management, technician workflows, and workshop administration.

Official product pages and product documentation are used as
primary evidence for the comparison.

---

## 1. AutoLeap

**Type:** Automotive repair shop management platform

**Official website:** https://autoleap.com/

AutoLeap provides a set of tools for managing automotive repair
shop operations.

Verified functionality includes:

- online appointment booking;
- service and time-slot selection;
- centralized scheduling;
- automated appointment reminders;
- customer communication;
- customer and vehicle history;
- technician assignment;
- technician mobile functionality;
- job/workflow tracking;
- digital inspections;
- payments;
- reporting and analytics.

AutoLeap also supports real-time scheduling and allows workshop
staff to view technician availability and assign jobs to service
bays and technicians.

### Relevance to Our Project

AutoLeap is highly relevant because many of its functions overlap
with the proposed system, particularly:

- online booking;
- vehicle records;
- appointment management;
- mechanic workflow;
- service history;
- administrator scheduling.

The system demonstrates that these functions are useful in a real
automotive service environment.

---

## 2. Tekmetric

**Type:** Cloud-based automotive shop management platform

**Official website:** https://www.tekmetric.com/

Tekmetric provides automotive workshop management and online
scheduling functionality.

Verified functionality includes:

- online service scheduling;
- real-time calendar integration;
- customizable shop availability;
- prevention of overbooking;
- automated appointment reminders;
- customer history and notes;
- digital vehicle inspections;
- role-based employee permissions;
- repair order management;
- reporting;
- inventory and parts management.

Customers can create appointments online, while workshop staff can
control business hours, unavailable periods, and scheduling
capacity.

### Relevance to Our Project

Tekmetric is especially relevant to:

- available time-slot management;
- booking conflict prevention;
- customer history;
- role-based permissions;
- workshop administration.

Its scheduling functionality demonstrates the importance of
considering actual workshop capacity rather than simply allowing
customers to choose any date and time.

---

## 3. Shopmonkey

**Type:** Automotive shop management software

**Official website:** https://www.shopmonkey.io/

Shopmonkey combines appointment scheduling with customer, vehicle,
work-order, and technician management.

Verified functionality includes:

- online appointment scheduling;
- selectable services;
- service duration configuration;
- pricing information;
- customer and vehicle information;
- prevention of double booking;
- workflow management;
- technician assignment;
- technician mobile application;
- vehicle details and history integrations;
- customer comments and internal notes;
- appointment confirmations and reminders.

The workflow system allows jobs to move through different stages,
while technicians can access assigned work orders from a mobile
application.

### Relevance to Our Project

Shopmonkey is relevant because its workflow resembles the proposed
Mechanic functionality.

Important similarities include:

- mechanic job lists;
- vehicle information;
- service information;
- job status changes;
- comments;
- appointment scheduling.

---

## 4. Shop-Ware

**Type:** Automotive repair shop management platform

**Official website:** https://shop-ware.com/

Shop-Ware provides scheduling, workflow, technician, customer, and
workshop management functionality.

Verified functionality includes:

- online service scheduling;
- real-time availability;
- scheduling rules;
- capacity management;
- prevention of overbooking;
- customer communication;
- automated reminders;
- technician job management;
- digital workflow;
- repair-order statuses;
- vehicle history;
- payments;
- reporting and analytics.

Technicians can access assigned jobs and vehicle information through
a dedicated interface.

### Relevance to Our Project

Shop-Ware is relevant to the proposed:

- online booking process;
- capacity-aware scheduling;
- mechanic dashboard;
- booking status workflow;
- administrator dashboard.

Its functionality also demonstrates the value of separating
customer-facing booking functionality from internal workshop
operations.

# Initial Observation

The analyzed systems confirm that online booking and workshop
management are established concepts in the automotive industry.

Therefore, the proposed diploma project does not claim that the
concept itself is unique.

Instead, the project aims to design and implement a manageable
web-based solution that demonstrates the complete software
engineering process and focuses on the core needs identified during
the user interviews:

- online appointment booking;
- visible available time slots;
- vehicle management;
- service catalogue;
- booking status tracking;
- service history;
- mechanic workflow;
- administrator scheduling and management.

The next stage of the analysis will compare all four systems using
the same evaluation criteria.
